Our 4-Step Editorial & Testing Protocol

Unlike automated scrape portals that mass-import broken or malware-laden files, G-Switch Games implements a rigorous 4-step quality assurance workflow before any game is published to our catalog:

1 Technical & Latency Audit

We measure time-to-interactive, frame rate stability (targeting 60 FPS), and keyboard/touch input response across standard school Chromebooks and low-spec machines.

2 Security & Code Inspection

Every HTML5 bundle is analyzed in a sandboxed environment to ensure it contains no malicious redirects, background miners, keyloggers, or unauthorized cookies.

3 Device & Cross-Browser Compatibility

We test full-screen scaling, WebGL rendering, and responsive viewport sizing across Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Apple Safari.

4 Content Appropriateness Review

We enforce strict standards regarding age-appropriateness, ensuring our puzzle, racing, action, and sports selections remain enjoyable and suitable for student breaks.

The G-Switch Heritage & Web Gaming Preservation

The inspiration behind this platform stems from the golden era of web gaming, spearheaded by visionary indie classics like G-Switch created by Portuguese developer Vasco Freitas (Serius Games). When Adobe Flash was officially deprecated, millions of iconic physics games faced permanent obsolescence.

Our team actively champions modern HTML5 conversions and lightweight WebAssembly ports, ensuring that masterworks like G-Switch 3, the Vex series by Amazing Adam, Fireboy & Watergirl, and Retro Bowl continue to be playable with zero plugins, zero lag, and 100% security for future generations.

Transparency & Community Inquiries
