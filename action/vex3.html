Vex 3

Description

Vex 3 is the groundbreaking stickman platformer that set the standard for modern speedrun parkour games. Leap across lethal spikes, bounce on trampolines, swing from zip-lines, and wall-jump through 10 challenging Acts and extreme challenge stages.

Controls:

WASD or Arrow Keys: Move, Jump, and Slide | W / Up Arrow: Jump / Wall-jump | S / Down Arrow: Crouch / Slide.

Tips and tricks:

  • Wall-jump by rapidly alternating left and right while sliding down walls.
  • Slide under low spinning blades by pressing down while in full sprint.
  • Touch every checkpoint pole so you respawn close to tricky obstacles.

About the creator:

Vex 3 was created by indie developer Amazing Adam.
