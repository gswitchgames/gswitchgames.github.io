Pro Tips & Winning Strategies for G Switch

  • Look Ahead, Not at Your Runner: The speed in G Switch 3 unblocked escalates rapidly. Focus your eyes on upcoming platforms and sawblades rather than tracking your avatar.
  • Switch Before, Not During: Invert gravity just as you reach the edge of an opening. Waiting until you are in the obstacle gap often results in falling into the abyss.
  • Master Clone Runner Splitting: In Clone mode, listen to the audio cues and establish a dual rhythm. Focus your attention on the lead runner while using peripheral vision for the trailing clone.
