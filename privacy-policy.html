User Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

At G-Switch Games, we believe in privacy by design. We do not require registration, do not collect personal identities, and strictly respect international data regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA.

Effective Date: October 2, 2026
Compliance: GDPR, CCPA & COPPA

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how G-Switch Games ("we", "us", or "our", accessible at gswitchgames.github.io) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit and play games on our website. We are dedicated to providing a safe, fun, and confidential gaming environment.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Unlike many modern platforms, G-Switch Games requires no user accounts, no login passwords, and no credit card details. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Payment card, billing, or financial information.
  • Social security numbers or government identifiers.

3. Information Collected Automatically (Log Data)

When you access our website, standard web server technology automatically logs non-identifiable technical data to maintain server performance and security. This may include:

  • Browser type, browser version, and operating system (e.g. ChromeOS, Windows, macOS).
  • Referring URLs and pages visited on our site.
  • Anonymized IP addresses used exclusively for geographic routing and server load balancing.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

We utilize browser cookies and HTML5 Local Storage to improve your gaming experience:

Local Storage (Game Saves)

Games like Vex, Fireboy & Watergirl, and Retro Bowl store level progression, high scores, and settings directly in your browser's local cache. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to external servers.

Google Analytics

We utilize Google Analytics (gtag.js) to monitor aggregate traffic trends, bounce rates, and popular games. This helps us optimize site performance. You may opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Advertising

To keep all games 100% free with unlimited play time, we partner with reputable third-party advertising networks (including Google AdSense). These third-party vendors may use cookies and web beacons to display relevant ads based on prior visits to our site or other internet locations.

You can manage personalized advertising preferences anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children's privacy is of paramount importance to us. In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly request, collect, or store personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has submitted personal data through our contact form, please notify us immediately at legal@gswitchgames.github.io and we will promptly delete all related records.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their data, including:

  • The right to know: Request disclosure of any automated diagnostic data collected.
  • The right to delete: Request erasure of any communication logs.
  • The right to non-discrimination: Equal access and gameplay experience regardless of privacy choices.

8. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact our team via email at privacy@gswitchgames.github.io or visit our Contact Page.
