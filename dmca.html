DMCA & Copyright Policy

G-Switch Games is committed to respecting and protecting intellectual property rights. We promptly investigate and respond to all legitimate copyright infringement notices in full compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

Designated Agent: legal@gswitchgames.github.io
Takedown SLA: 24–48 Hours

1. Commitment to Creators

G-Switch Games operates as an online web index and educational curation service for HTML5 browser games. We deeply value the creative energy and commercial rights of independent game creators, publishers, and developers. All game trademarks, character designs, game logos, and source code belong to their respective original owners.

We only index games that have been made publicly available across web distribution channels or provided with open embed permissions. If any content on this website infringes upon your copyright, we will swiftly remove or disable access to the infringing material.

2. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file an official copyright infringement notification with G-Switch Games, please send a written communication to our Designated Copyright Agent containing the following statutory elements:

1. Identification of Work

A detailed description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed, including registration numbers if applicable.

2. Exact Location URL

The exact URL(s) on gswitchgames.github.io where the disputed game or material is located so we can locate it immediately.

3. Contact Information

Your full legal name, company name (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and official email address.

4. Legal Statements & Signature

A statement of good faith belief, statement under penalty of perjury of your authority, and an electronic or physical signature.

3. Designated Copyright Agent Contact

Please direct all official DMCA notices to our designated legal inbox:

Email: legal@gswitchgames.github.io / dmca@gswitchgames.github.io
Review Hours: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (UTC)

4. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a formal Counter-Notification pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g). Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, we will provide a copy to the original complaining party.
