Car Speed Racing Tycoon

Description

Car Speed Racing Tycoon is an exhilarating 3D racing game where players build their automotive empire from the ground up. Start with a basic car and earn cash through races to upgrade your ride or purchase new vehicles. Compete in various race types across different tracks, master drifting techniques, and use nitro boosts strategically to outpace opponents. As you progress, unlock new garages, hire mechanics, and expand your collection to become the ultimate racing tycoon. Can you dominate the tracks and build the most successful racing empire?

Controls:

WASD / Arrow Keys: Drive & Steer | Spacebar / Shift: Handbrake / Drift | N Key: Nitro Boost.

Tips and tricks:

  • You can play multiplayer with real people or AIs.
  • Try to switch just before an obstacle rather than when you hit it.
  • You can use the mouse or keyboard to play. Find what works for you!

About the creator:
