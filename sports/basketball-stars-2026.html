Basketball Stars 2026

Description

Basketball Stars 2026 brings fast-paced street basketball action with new teams, tournament brackets, and electrifying slam dunks on futuristic courts.

Controls:

Player 1: WASD (Move) + B (Shoot/Steal) + V (Super) | Player 2: Arrow Keys + L (Shoot/Steal) + K (Super)

Tips and tricks:

  • Execute quick crossovers to break through aggressive man-to-man defense.
  • Activate the 2026 turbo boost when driving down the baseline.
  • Coordinate alley-oops in 2-player co-op mode.

About the creator:

Basketball Stars 2026 was developed by Madpuffers.
